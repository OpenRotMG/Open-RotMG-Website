Export save

Download your whole personal account to keep a copy of your progress. This is not a selected-character export. Your live account stays intact.

The file excludes live login credentials, but it is not anonymized. Names, artwork and death descriptions can identify you. Keep it private.

Before you export

Leave the game on every device and wait for your character to finish saving. Use your registered game email and password below. Guest accounts cannot export.

Your credentials are sent only to the game server for this request. This page does not store them.

Using your save offline

Offline import uses a new local password, not your live password. Local progress does not sync back to the live game. See the single-player preview for availability.

This file is personal progress, not a full local backup. Functioning guild membership, social relationships, trade receipts, rankings and external integrations do not transfer. Historical guild contribution and Discord Realm Close counts are inert history, not working offline features.
