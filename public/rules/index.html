Rules

  • Open RotMG is a time machine, not a remake server. We want everybody to have fun, especially players just here to enjoy the game and role-play like it's 2012.
  • There is deliberately no concrete rulebook. We care about the spirit, not loopholes.
  • Spoiling others' fun can mean silent nerfs, account deletion or bans without warning.
  • This policy can change with feedback. The point is to let people enjoy their trip back to 2012.

Cheating

  • Primitive autoaim and autonexus are era-authentic, not consequence-free: expect a modest loot penalty.
  • Godmode, speed cheats and full botting go beyond that era.
  • Our custom classifiers detect cheating. Penalties can be delayed; full botting can mean no white bags or random disconnects.
  • Nerfs can wear off after time playing clean, but a cheating mark on the hiscores is permanent.
