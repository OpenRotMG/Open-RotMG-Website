About Open RotMG

Open RotMG is an authentic recreation of Realm of the Mad God as it was in 2012. No custom content, no donations, all open source. Development is from passionate players.

What works today?

A shared development world supports guest Wizard movement, combat, potions, loot, equipment, death and saved reconnects. The full 2012 game is still being rebuilt. See Restoration for progress by feature.
