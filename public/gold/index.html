Open RotMG Gold

Open RotMG is free to play forever. No P2W. Gold is earned by doing things in and outside the game.

How to get Gold

One-time methods

  • Link your Discord account: 100 Gold. Type /discord in game, then use /link code:your-private-code on Discord.
  • Get into the top 50% of the Collection Log hiscores: 100 Gold. Collect at least one item. Ranks include ties, with the top half rounded up. Checked when you add a new collection entry.
  • Submit your first bug report: 50 Gold. Type /bug description in game. Gold is awarded when your report is posted to GitHub.
  • Make a YouTube video about openrotmg.org: 100 Gold.

Repeatable methods

  • 100 Gold per 1,000 views on a YouTube video about Open RotMG.
  • 50 Gold per 1,000 views for a Short or a video on another social media platform.

Discord linking, collection and first bug report rewards are automatic, once per game account. You will get an in-game chat notification when Gold is granted. If you are offline, it will appear when you next enter the game.

Video rewards are reviewed and granted manually. Share your video on Discord.

Have more suggestions for Gold giveaways? Post on Discord.
