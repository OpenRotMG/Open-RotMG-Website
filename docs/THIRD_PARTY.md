# Third-party artwork

The Restoration and hiscore collection sprites are exact crops from the preserved 123.5.1 Flash client. Hiscores uses a small atlas of the current enemy-drop items; silhouettes are a CSS filter, not replacement artwork. The recovered client originates from these preservation repositories:

https://github.com/ViciousFate/rotmg-redis

https://github.com/trapped/rotmg_svr

https://github.com/lcnvdl/rotmg-server

The Branding page publishes the owner's Oryx organization logo and combined desktop/mobile banners from the GitHub organization README. Banner and social-image backgrounds depict historical Realm of the Mad God gameplay, not the current Open RotMG development build. The amulet icon is preserved game artwork.

The six historical screenshots are frames from these 2012 YouTube uploads; the Branding page credits the uploaders and links each capture timestamp:

- GunnyBobbunny, March 19: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vyvum_qPaw8
- Le Rush, April 27: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p0oD-0YdM_c
- LPZ, June 28: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trj_Xvxf5Go
- 2MunchGames, September 16: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xyUiQewDLg8

Only black side bars were cropped from the train frame. No frames were upscaled or retouched. Upload dates do not establish the exact recorded client build; these are original Realm footage, not Open RotMG development screenshots. The owner explicitly requested publication of these six frames. That request is not a license grant from the video creators.

Original game artwork remains third-party material. This website's GPL license does not grant rights from its original rightsholders. Publication records the project owner's stated permission to preserve and publish the recovered material, not an independent legal determination.
